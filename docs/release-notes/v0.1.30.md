## What's new

- **Fixes "Skipped 2 item(s) that didn't pass checks".** WoW Forever stopped telling add-ons the realm name, so the app was skipping every character's save and your progress didn't go out. An empty realm is fine now; the app keeps the realm it already knew. After updating, /reload or log out once to share your progress.
- When a sync skips something, Activity now says what and why (for example "bad realm name"), and **Copy support details** in Settings lists it too. Before, it only said how many.
- Realm names with unusual punctuation no longer stop a character from syncing either; the app tidies the name.
- The skip message is only added again when the reason changes, so Activity doesn't fill up with repeats.
- New app versions show on Home within the hour (syncs now look for one), instead of up to 6 hours later.

## Install or update

- **Already have the app (0.1.24 or newer):** it offers this update on Home, or click **Check for updates** in Settings.
- **New, or on 0.1.23 or older:** download `SterlingQuestSync_0.1.30_x64-setup.exe` below and run it. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Your settings and partners stay.
- The Companion is optional and works with the [Sterling Quest Sync](https://github.com/SterlingAddons/SterlingQuest) add-on. Setup, troubleshooting and privacy: [the app's page](https://github.com/SterlingAddons/SterlingQuestSync-App#readme).

`latest.json` and the `.sig` file are for the app's own updater; you don't need to download them.
